About Kunyarungo AgriTech Solutions

Empowering farmers through sustainable technology

Our Organisation

Kunyarungo AgriTech Solutions is an agritech enterprise based in rural South Africa, dedicated to empowering small-scale and emerging farmers with sustainable agricultural solutions. Our name, "Kunyarungo," reflects our commitment to growing together with the farming communities we serve.

Founded on the principle that technology should be accessible to all farmers regardless of their scale of operation, we design and supply affordable, solar-powered irrigation and water management systems that help farmers reduce reliance on unpredictable rainfall and rising electricity costs.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To empower small-scale and emerging farmers with affordable, sustainable agricultural technology that improves crop yield and water security.

Our Vision

To become a leading provider of accessible, renewable energy agricultural solutions across Southern Africa, enabling farmers to build resilient, profitable, and sustainable operations.

Our History & Growth

1

Foundation

Kunyarungo AgriTech Solutions was established to address the critical challenge of water scarcity and rising energy costs in rural farming communities.

2

Product Development

We developed our first generation of solar-powered irrigation kits, tested and refined with feedback from local farming cooperatives.

3

Strategic Partnerships

We established partnerships with government agencies (DALRRD) and NGOs to expand our reach and support government-funded agricultural initiatives.

4

Expansion & Impact

Today, we serve small-scale farmers, agricultural cooperatives, and development organisations across South Africa, with plans to expand to the broader Southern African region.

Our Target Audience

We serve several key segments in the agricultural and development sectors:

  • Small-scale and emerging farmers looking to improve water security and reduce energy costs
  • Agricultural cooperatives seeking scalable, sustainable farming solutions for their members
  • Government agencies (such as DALRRD) funding sustainable agricultural initiatives
  • NGOs and development partners supporting rural economic development and food security
